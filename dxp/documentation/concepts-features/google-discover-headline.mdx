**Google Discover Headline** is an opt-in field in Post Details, positioned directly below **Title**. Once enabled for your account, it carries the same data and purpose as the OG Title field you may already know — just renamed and relocated so it's visible while you write, instead of tucked inside SEO Properties.

## Enabling it

Google Discover Headline is opt-in — email [support@thepublive.com](mailto:support@thepublive.com) to have it enabled for your account. There's no setup required beyond that request.

<Note>
  If you don't request this, nothing changes in your dashboard. OG Title stays exactly where it is today, in SEO Properties, working exactly as it always has.
</Note>

## What changes

Once enabled, your existing OG Title value carries over into the new field automatically — nothing to re-enter. The OG Title input is then removed from the SEO Properties panel; every other field there (Meta Title, OG Description, Twitter Title) stays exactly as it was.

## Why it matters

Google Discover Headline controls one specific thing: the headline that appears when your content shows up in Google Discover. It's not your page's Title (H1), and it's not your Meta Title — those are separate fields with separate jobs. Grouped inside SEO Properties alongside several similarly named fields, that distinction was easy to miss.

[Google's own Discover documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-discover) calls out compelling, non-misleading titles as a factor in whether content gets picked up by the feed — which is why this field now has a dedicated, visible spot next to Title.

## Using the field

* Hover the **ⓘ** icon on either **Title** or **Google Discover Headline** for a quick explanation of how each is used.
* If left blank, Google Discover shows your **Title** instead.
* A live character counter shows as you type. We recommend keeping it under approximately 70 characters so it displays cleanly in Discover — there's no hard limit, and longer headlines still save.
* The AI-generate (sparkle) icon next to the field works the same way it does on Title.

## Scope

Google Discover Headline is available on every content type that previously supported OG Title, including Template Pages. It's not available on Custom Content → Blank Page.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my existing OG Title value if I enable this?">
    No. Once enabled, your existing OG Title value is carried over into the new Google Discover Headline field automatically — nothing to re-enter or re-save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does this affect my Meta Title, Twitter Title, or my page's H1?">
    No, whether or not you enable this. Google Discover Headline only controls what was previously OG Title. Meta Title, Twitter Title, structured data, and your page's H1 are all untouched.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find OG Title in SEO Properties anymore — is it gone?">
    OG Title only moves out of SEO Properties once Google Discover Headline is enabled for your account. If you haven't requested it, OG Title is exactly where it's always been.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
