AXP Edge connects at the CDN layer. This page shows the steps from start to go-live, and links to the guide for your CDN.

## Prerequisites

- Admin access to your domain's DNS or nameserver records.
- Access to your CDN or edge network configuration.
- A Publive AXP account with AXP Edge enabled for your domain. [Get AXP Edge](https://axp.thepublive.com/contact).

<Note>
  **No CDN yet?** AXP Edge needs a CDN in front of your domain to route AI agent traffic. We recommend [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/cdn/), because it is the fastest path to go live. Publive can set it up for you or help your team: [contact Publive](https://axp.thepublive.com/contact).
</Note>

## Go live in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get set up">
    Publive gives you **AXP Edge enabled for your domain**, your **Edge CDS API key**, and your **backend URL**. If you do not have these, [contact Publive](https://axp.thepublive.com/contact). You give your team admin access to your CDN. Make sure that your CDN, `robots.txt`, and WAF do not block `PubliveBot/1.0` or the AI agent user-agents (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and similar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy your integration">
    Open the [integration guide](#integration-guides) for your CDN and follow its steps. Each guide adds the routing (a Worker, middleware, a function, or CDN rules) and the Publive configuration values. Keep the API key in your CDN's secret store where it has one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and monitor">
    Use the Verify steps in your guide to check that CDS serves AI agent requests and that human traffic does not change. Then use the [AXP dashboard](/axp/axp-edge/dashboard) to set rules and watch AI agent traffic.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  To roll back, remove the AXP Edge routing from your CDN. All traffic, including AI agent traffic, then goes to your normal origin. Some guides have a Rollback section with the exact steps.
</Note>

To keep some pages or sections out of the AI agent view, use [URL pattern overrides](/axp/axp-edge/dashboard#url-pattern-overrides) on the **Global Rules** tab.
