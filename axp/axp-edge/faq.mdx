---
title: "AXP Edge FAQ"
description: "Frequently asked questions about how AXP Edge works."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this cloaking?">
    No. AXP Edge only cleans up, restructures, and re-represents what is already on your page, so AI agents and your visitors get the same substance. Cloaking shows search engines different content to manipulate rankings; AXP Edge serves the same facts in a machine-readable form to crawlers that ask for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will it change what my customers see?">
    Nothing changes for customers or Google. Only AI tools get the cleaned-up version; humans and search engines are routed to your site exactly as it is today.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will it hurt my existing SEO or rankings?">
    No. Googlebot and other search crawlers continue to receive your normal pages, so your existing SEO is fully preserved. AXP Edge only changes what AI agents receive.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    Under two hours — one small install on the CDN you already use, with one-click rollback. No site rebuild and no code changes to your application.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which CDNs and platforms are supported?">
    Cloudflare, Amazon CloudFront, and Akamai, among others. If you serve your site through a CDN or edge network, AXP Edge can almost certainly integrate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if something breaks on your side?">
    It fails safe. If the edge layer is ever unavailable, requests fall straight back to your normal origin, so visitors always get your real site.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it slow down my site or add latency?">
    No. Real visitors hit your origin exactly as before. The optimized view is generated for AI agents only, so human page speed and Core Web Vitals are unaffected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do you decide who gets the agent view?">
    AXP Edge detects AI crawlers and agents by user-agent and network signals. Humans, and traditional search bots you want to preserve, are passed through to your normal site untouched.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from AI Streams?">
    AXP Edge makes the pages you already have readable to AI. AI Streams (coming soon) builds new, AI-first content on your domain to expand what you can be cited for. Many brands run both.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
