---
title: "Don't have a CDN?"
description: "How to get set up with AXP Edge when your site isn't behind a CDN yet."
---

AXP Edge integrates at the CDN layer, so it works with any site already served through one. If your site isn't behind a CDN yet, you'll need one in front of your domain before AXP Edge can route agent traffic.

We recommend [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/cdn/), since it has the fastest path to going live with AXP Edge. Publive can set it up for you or assist your team through the setup: [contact us](https://axp.thepublive.com/contact).
