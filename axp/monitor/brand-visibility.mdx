title: "Brand Visibility"
description: "See how your brand performs across all tracked prompts, against competitors, and by topic."
---

**Brand Visibility** is the brand-wide view of prompt monitoring. It combines the data from all your tracked prompts and competitors. It is in the **Monitor** group of the AXP dashboard sidebar.

## Filters

- **Date range:** The default range is the last 30 days.
- **Filter agent:** Show the data for all platforms or for one platform.

Brand Visibility counts only the responses to prompts that do not mention your brand. A prompt that mentions your brand almost always gets a response that mentions your brand, so it does not show how visible you are.

## Headline metrics

Five cards show the main metrics for the date range.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **AI visibility** | The percentage of answered responses that mention your brand |
| **Sentiment score** | How positive the responses are about your brand |
| **Share of voice** | Your brand mentions as a percentage of all brand mentions across all prompts |
| **Citations** | The number of times the responses cite your domains |
| **Citation rate** | The number of inline citations of your domains, divided by the number of answered responses |

Point to the info icon on a card to see its definition. For more information, refer to the [metrics and fields reference](/axp/monitor/metrics).

## Visibility over time

A chart shows your brand and its top competitors for each day in the date range. Select a metric to change the chart:

- **Visibility**
- **Sentiment**
- **Share of Voice**
- **Position**
- **Citations**

A break in a line means that there were no answered responses on that day. For position, the chart uses the average of all responses on each day.

## Top performing brands

A table under the chart shows how the top brands perform in the responses to your prompts. Each row shows visibility, share of voice, position, citations, citation rate, and sentiment.

## Brand leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks brands against each other by one metric. Each row shows the value of that metric for the brand.

Select the metric for the ranking:

| Metric | Ranks first |
|---|---|
| **Visibility** (default) | The brand that the highest percentage of answered responses mention |
| **Sentiment** | The brand with the highest sentiment score |
| **Share of Voice** | The brand with the largest share of all brand mentions |
| **Position** | The brand with the lowest average position. Brands with no position go to the end. |
| **Citations** | The brand with the most citations of its domains |

AXP ranks all brands before it shows the top of the list. So a brand with few mentions can still lead the leaderboard for position.

## Head-to-head

Compare your brand with one competitor at a time. Select a different competitor to change the comparison. The table compares share of voice, AI visibility, citations, sentiment, and average position. Sentiment shows as a label: **Positive**, **Neutral**, or **Negative**.

## Brand insights

Brand insights shows your performance by topic, so you can see where you are strong or weak.

- Each topic row shows **Visibility**, **Sentiment**, **Share of Voice**, **Position**, **Mentions**, and **Citations** for the prompts in the topic. **Mentions** shows the brands as logos, with the number of other brands.
- Expand a topic to see the list of its prompts.
- Click the view icon (**View tracked prompts**) on a topic to open the [prompt list](/axp/monitor/track-prompts#topics), filtered to that topic. To see the [analytics](/axp/monitor/prompt-analytics) of a prompt, click the prompt in that list.
- You can search the table. You can also download it.
- Click **Customise columns** to select the columns that the table shows.

## Charts

Every chart on this page has these controls:

- Point to the info icon to see what the chart measures.
- Point to a legend item to show its line clearly. The other lines become dim.
- Download the chart as a PNG image.
