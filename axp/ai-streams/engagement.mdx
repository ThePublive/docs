Every AI Streams page moves through a fixed pipeline of AI agents grounded in your Brand Knowledge Hub, with a human review step before anything publishes.

## The six steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Onboard & brand context">
    A Publive CSM builds your Living Brand profile: products, services, ideal customer profile, regions, voice, and compliance requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Curate the prompt set">
    The Prompt Curator Agent shortlists prompts by citation gap, buyer demand, and competition, and shares a baseline Citations % before anything is written. You approve the prompt set before content production starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the Brand Knowledge Hub">
    Built against your website, URLs, PDFs, and videos, and validated by you. This is the only source content generation draws from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy content agents">
    AI agents create content from the Brand Knowledge Hub, grounded in your approved sources and brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Human review, approval, publish">
    Every page is human-vetted, fact-checked, then client-approved on the Publive CMS before it publishes to `/streams`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report & refresh">
    Monthly AI visibility reporting, plus agent-led refresh and optimization to keep pages relevant as AI answers change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to expect, and when

| Timeframe | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1-4 | Foundation: onboarding, prompt set locked, Brand Knowledge Hub set up. |
| Months 2-3 | Build: content produced, approved, published, and discovered by answer engines. |
| Months 4-6 | Citation movement: Citations % and Share of Voice climb, with the first benchmark review at month 6. |

## The content agents

Every agent below is grounded in your Brand Knowledge Hub, and every page clears human review before it ships.

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| Prompt Curator Agent | Shortlists prompts by citation gap, buyer demand, and competition. |
| Content Builder Agent | Creates structured pages around selected prompts, sourced from your Brand Knowledge Hub. |
| Design & Builder Agent | Builds page types and components from the CMS, grounded in your brand variables. |
| SEO & Compliance QA Agent | Validates SEO, schema, internal links, compliance, and brand voice. |
| Competition Check Agent | Tracks competitor content in AI citations to keep the bar current. |
| Content Refresh Agent | Keeps pages relevant as AI answers change, through continuous optimization. |
